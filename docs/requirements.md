# AI Recruiting Assistant

## Problem

College coaches receive large volumes of recruiting emails and spend significant time identifying prospects that fit their program.

## Solution

An AI-powered recruiting inbox that extracts player information from emails and scores prospects against coach-defined recruiting criteria.

## Users

- College Coaches
- Recruiting Coordinators

## Core Features

- Email ingestion
- AI prospect extraction
- Prospect database
- AI fit scoring
- Search and filtering

## Future Features

- Resume parsing
- Video analysis
- Weekly recruiting reports

## Success Metrics

- Reduce email review time
- Improve prospect identification
- Surface top-fit players automatically
